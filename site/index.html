A little context.

An independent map of productions in Cirque du Soleil’s official show catalogue. Current runs describe a residency or tour engagement, not a guarantee of a performance every day.

Prices are advertised ticket prices and published ranges, where available. “From” means the upper end is unpublished. Discounts apply only to eligible seats, dates and ticket categories; we never apply a headline percentage to a starting price to invent a promotional price. Taxes and fees follow the conditions shown for each offer. Checkout is authoritative.

Offers retain their full descriptions under each show. A group discount, child price, upgrade, and full admission ticket have different conditions. The “With offers” filter includes these categories.

Data refresh is scheduled daily via GitHub Actions. A failed refresh preserves the last successful snapshot and its original timestamp. Expired touring runs leave the current view automatically. Resident availability and offers require a successful refresh.
